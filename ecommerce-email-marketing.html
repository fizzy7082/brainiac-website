Ecommerce email marketing

Ecommerce email marketing and automation that keeps customers coming back

Most first-time visitors leave without buying, and many customers buy once and never return. Automated emails keep those relationships going: a welcome for new subscribers, a reminder when a cart is left behind, and a reason to come back after the first order.

The problem

Problems this service solves

Abandoned carts with no follow-up

Shoppers who reach the cart are showing real interest. Without an abandoned cart email flow, that interest disappears.

One-time buyers

Without post-purchase and win-back emails, customers who liked your product simply forget about you.

Campaigns sent to everyone

Sending the same message to every subscriber lowers engagement and increases unsubscribes.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Klaviyo?

We usually recommend Klaviyo for ecommerce because of its store integrations, but we can work with other email platforms if you already use one. The platform subscription is paid directly by you.

Do abandoned cart emails need a discount?

No. Many stores start with a simple reminder and helpful information. Whether to add an incentive is a business decision we'll discuss with you.

How often should we send campaigns?

It depends on your products and audience. We agree a calendar you can sustain and adjust based on engagement.

Are the emails compliant with privacy rules?

We set up consent-based sign-up forms and unsubscribe links. You remain responsible for your list and privacy policy, and we'll flag anything that needs attention.
