Online Store Design and Development

Book a Consultation

The problem

Many online stores run on a template that was never adapted to their products. Navigation is confusing, product pages leave questions unanswered and the store feels less trustworthy than the brand behind it.

What's included

  • New store builds and redesigns of existing stores
  • Theme selection, setup and customization
  • Homepage, collection and product page layouts
  • Navigation and menu structure
  • App selection and setup for reviews, subscriptions and upsells
  • Mobile-first responsive design

Key deliverables

  • A live, tested online store or redesign
  • Page templates you can edit yourself
  • A short handover guide for your team
  • A post-launch check of key pages and checkout

Potential business benefits

A clearer store helps shoppers find products faster and trust what they are buying, which supports more completed orders and a stronger brand image.

Typical timeline

3 to 6 weeks, depending on the number of pages and custom features.

What we need from you

  • Admin or staff access to your store platform
  • Logo, brand colors and fonts
  • Product photos and descriptions
  • Examples of stores you like

How it works

  1. Brief and goals
  2. Layout and design
  3. Build and test
  4. Launch and handover

Ecommerce Store Audits

Book a Consultation

The problem

When a store gets visitors but not enough sales, it is hard to see the cause from the inside. An outside review shows where shoppers get stuck.

What's included

  • Usability review on desktop and mobile
  • Product page and collection page review
  • Cart and checkout review
  • Technical issues: speed, broken links, app conflicts
  • Trust signals: reviews, policies, contact details
  • Basic SEO and email setup check

Key deliverables

  • A written audit report with screenshots
  • Findings ranked by likely impact and effort
  • Clear, practical recommendations
  • A walkthrough call to go over the findings

Potential business benefits

You get a prioritized list of what to fix first, so time and budget go to the changes most likely to make a difference.

Typical timeline

5 to 7 business days after we receive access.

What we need from you

  • Your store URL and collaborator access
  • Store analytics or Google Analytics access, if available
  • Your main goals and any known problems

How it works

  1. Access and goals
  2. Store review
  3. Written report
  4. Walkthrough call

Product Page Optimization

Book a Consultation

The problem

Your product page is where shoppers decide. When photos are out of order, the copy lists features instead of benefits, or sizing, delivery and returns are hard to find, shoppers leave to compare elsewhere.

What's included

  • Review of your best-selling product pages
  • Photo order and gallery improvements
  • Benefit-led product titles and descriptions
  • Size, delivery and returns information near Add to cart
  • Reviews, guarantees and trust signals placement
  • Mobile layout and sticky Add to cart

Key deliverables

  • Rewritten copy for the agreed products
  • An improved product page template
  • Before-and-after screenshots of each change
  • Recommendations for your remaining products

Potential business benefits

Clearer product pages answer shoppers' questions before they ask, which can help more of your existing visitors add to cart and buy.

Typical timeline

2 to 3 weeks for up to 10 products.

What we need from you

  • Admin or staff access to your store
  • Product photos, specs and size charts
  • Your shipping and returns policies
  • Any customer reviews you already have

How it works

  1. Pick key products
  2. Review and plan
  3. Rewrite and redesign
  4. Launch and review

Conversion Rate Optimization

Book a Consultation

The problem

Getting traffic is expensive. If shoppers struggle to navigate, compare products or complete checkout, much of that traffic is wasted.

What's included

  • Navigation and search improvements
  • Product page layout, imagery and copy
  • Trust signals: reviews, guarantees, delivery info
  • Mobile shopping experience
  • Cart and checkout usability
  • A/B testing where traffic allows

Key deliverables

  • A prioritized list of improvements
  • Design and implementation of agreed changes
  • Test plans and results where tests are run

Small improvements at each step of the funnel can add up, helping more of your existing visitors become customers.

3 to 6 weeks, plus testing time where traffic allows.

  • Permission to publish agreed changes

How it works

  2. Find friction
  3. Implement changes
  4. Measure and repeat

Ecommerce SEO

Book a Consultation

The problem

Products with thin descriptions, duplicate titles and unstructured collections are hard for search engines to understand, so the store depends on paid traffic.

What's included

  • Keyword research for products and collections
  • Product and collection titles, descriptions and image alt text
  • Meta titles and meta descriptions
  • Collection structure and internal linking
  • Technical checks: indexing, redirects, site speed basics
  • Structured data for products

Key deliverables

  • Keyword map for your main pages
  • Optimized product and collection content
  • Technical SEO fix list, with completed fixes marked
  • A plain-language summary of changes

Potential business benefits

Better organic visibility can bring more qualified shoppers over time without paying for every click. SEO takes time, and no one can guarantee specific rankings, so we focus on doing the groundwork properly.

Typical timeline

2 to 4 weeks for the initial work. Rankings usually take months to move.

What we need from you

  • Admin or staff access to your store
  • Google Search Console access, or permission to set it up
  • Your priority products and collections

How it works

  1. SEO review
  2. Keyword map
  3. On-page updates
  4. Monitor and refine

What we need from you

  • Klaviyo account (on your plan)
  • Admin or staff access to your store
  • Logo, brand colors and product photos
  • Any offers or discounts you want to use

How it works

  2. Flow plan
  3. Build and test
  4. Report and improve

Facebook and Instagram Advertising

Book a Consultation

The problem

Ad budgets disappear quickly when tracking is broken, audiences are too broad or creative is not tested. It becomes hard to tell what is working.

What's included

  • Meta Pixel and Conversions API setup checks
  • Audience strategy: prospecting and retargeting
  • Campaign structure and budget planning
  • Ad creative direction and copy
  • Ongoing testing and optimization

Key deliverables

  • A campaign plan with budgets and audiences
  • Live campaigns in Meta Ads Manager
  • Weekly or monthly performance reports
  • Recommendations for the next round of tests

Potential business benefits

Well-structured campaigns with reliable tracking make your ad spend easier to measure and improve. Results depend on product, offer and market, so we do not promise a fixed return on ad spend.

Typical timeline

1 to 2 weeks to set up and launch, then ongoing management.

What we need from you

  • Meta Business Manager and ad account access
  • A monthly ad budget, paid directly to Meta
  • Product photos or videos for ads
  • Your margins, so we can set sensible targets

How it works

  1. Tracking check
  2. Campaign plan
  3. Launch
  4. Test and optimize

eBay and TikTok Shop Integration

Book a Consultation

The problem

Relying on one sales channel limits reach. New marketplaces bring new shoppers, but each has its own rules, listing formats and fees.

What's included

  • Channel fit assessment for your products
  • eBay and TikTok Shop setup and connection to your store
  • Product listing creation and optimization
  • Inventory and order sync setup
  • Channel policy and compliance guidance

Key deliverables

  • A channel recommendation
  • Live, connected marketplace storefronts
  • Optimized product listings
  • A simple guide to managing orders

Potential business benefits

Selling on the right additional channels can reach shoppers who would not find your own store, while keeping inventory and orders in one place.

Typical timeline

2 to 4 weeks, including marketplace account approval.

What we need from you

  • Admin or staff access to your store
  • Product data, stock levels and shipping rules

How it works

  1. Fit check
  2. Account setup
  3. Listings and sync
  4. Launch and review
