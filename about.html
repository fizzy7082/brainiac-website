About us

Helping Ecommerce Businesses Build, Improve, and Grow.

Brainiac Marketing Agency works with online store owners, ecommerce retailers and growing brands. We focus on four things: building strong stores, bringing in the right customers, improving how the store converts and making every customer's experience better.

Who we are

An agency built around online stores

We work on ecommerce. That focus means we understand how shoppers move from a first visit to a repeat order, and which parts of a store, its marketing and its emails affect each step.

We combine online store development, SEO, email automation, social media, paid advertising and conversion optimization, so you can work with one team that sees the whole picture.

Mission

Make growth practical

To give ecommerce businesses clear strategies and skilled execution that help them build better stores and grow sustainably.

Vision

Every store at its best

A world where independent online businesses can compete with the biggest brands through smart design, honest marketing and good customer experience.

Core values

How we work with every client

Understand first

We learn about your business before recommending anything.

Be transparent

Clear scopes, honest timelines and plain-language reporting.

Decide with data

We use your store's real numbers to guide decisions and check results.

Respect the customer

Good marketing helps shoppers make confident decisions. We avoid tricks and pressure tactics.

Keep improving

Deliver what we promise
