How to Create Product Pages That Help Customers Buy

By the Brainiac Marketing Agency team · November 2, 2026 · 9 min read

In a physical shop, customers can pick a product up, try it and ask a question. Online, the product page has to do all of that. A good product page answers the questions a shopper would ask a knowledgeable salesperson, in the order they'd ask them.

Start with what the shopper needs to know

Before rewriting anything, list the questions customers ask before buying. Good sources are customer service emails, live chat transcripts, reviews (including negative ones) and returns reasons. These questions become the structure of the page.

Photos: show the product clearly first

  • Make the first image a clear view of the product on its own.
  • Follow with key angles, close-ups of materials or details, and the product in use.
  • Show scale: the product next to something familiar or on a person.
  • For variants, show every color or finish, and switch the image when a variant is selected.
  • Compress images and use modern formats so they load quickly on mobile.

Title: clear before clever

The product title should say what the product is, using the words shoppers search for, plus the most important distinguishing detail such as material, size or model. A creative product name can stay, but pair it with a description: "The Weekend Tote – Waxed Canvas Bag" tells shoppers and search engines far more than "The Weekend".

Price, options and Add to cart above the fold

On mobile, a shopper should see the product, price, key options and Add to cart without scrolling far. If there are discounts, bundles or subscription options, explain them simply. Make the selected size or color obvious, and show clearly when an option is out of stock.

Answer delivery and returns next to the button

Unexpected costs are one of the most common reasons shoppers abandon, according to Baymard Institute research. Put a short line about delivery cost and timing, and your returns policy, right next to Add to cart, with a link to the full details. Shoppers shouldn't have to leave the page to find them.

Write descriptions around benefits and specifics

A strong product description usually has three parts:

  1. The opening: one or two sentences on who the product is for and the main benefit.
  2. Key benefits: a short scannable list connecting features to outcomes ("Waxed canvas, so light rain wipes off").
  3. The details: materials, dimensions, care instructions, what's in the box and compatibility.

Write originally. Supplier descriptions are often used by many stores, which gives search engines no reason to prefer your page. Original copy helps both ecommerce SEO and conversion.

Help shoppers choose the right size or variant

For clothing, footwear and furniture, sizing doubts lead to abandoned carts and returns. Offer a size guide on the page, explain fit ("runs small, size up"), and give measurements in the units your customers use.

Show genuine social proof

Real customer reviews answer questions the brand can't answer convincingly itself. Show the average rating and number of reviews near the title and the full reviews further down. Include photos from customers if you have them, respond to negative reviews constructively, and never write or buy fake reviews.

Use structured data correctly

Product structured data helps Google read price, availability and genuine review ratings. Only mark up what's visible on the page, and keep prices and stock in sync. Google's documentation on structured data for ecommerce explains the supported properties.

Design for mobile first

  • Use a sticky Add to cart bar once the main button scrolls out of view.
  • Use collapsible sections for long details, but keep delivery and returns visible.
  • Make buttons and option selectors large enough to tap easily.
  • Avoid pop-ups that cover the product on arrival.

Product page checklist

  • Clear first image, full set of angles and scale
  • Descriptive title with the product type
  • Price, options and Add to cart visible on mobile
  • Delivery cost, delivery time and returns next to the button
  • Benefit-led opening and scannable key benefits
  • Full specifications, care and sizing information
  • Genuine reviews and ratings
  • Unique meta title, meta description and image alt text
  • Correct product structured data
  • Fast loading on a mobile connection
