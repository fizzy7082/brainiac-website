Before we start

Everything is agreed in writing first

Your proposal spells out each of these before work begins, so there are no surprises.

Scope

Exactly which pages, flows and tasks are included, and what is not.

Number of products

How many products or collections we will optimize. Larger catalogs are quoted separately.

Revision rounds

How many rounds of changes are included before the work is signed off.

Delivery timeline

Start date, milestones and delivery date, based on the agreed scope.

Third-party subscriptions

Shopify plans, apps, themes and tools like Klaviyo are paid directly by you and are not included in our fees.

Advertising spend

Any ad budget for Meta or other platforms is separate from our fees and paid directly to the platform.

Questions

Pricing questions

Why do packages say "starting from"?

Every store is different. The starting price covers the listed deliverables for a typical store. If your catalog is large or you need extra work, we quote it in your proposal before you commit.

Can I combine or customize packages?

Yes. Tell us what you need and we'll build a proposal around it, using these packages as a guide.

Do you guarantee results?

No one can honestly guarantee rankings, sales or ad returns. We guarantee to deliver the agreed work to a professional standard and to explain what we did and why.

How do we get started?

Book a free consultation or message us on WhatsApp. We'll learn about your store, recommend a package and send a written proposal.

Not sure which package fits?

Tell us about your store and goals, and we'll recommend the right starting point.
