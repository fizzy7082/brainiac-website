Questions

Pricing questions

Why do packages say "from"?

Every store is different. The starting price covers the listed deliverables for a typical store. If your catalog is larger or you need extra work, we quote it in your proposal before you commit.

Can I pay in stages?

Yes. Projects are usually split into a deposit to start and the balance at delivery. Larger packages can be split into milestones. The payment plan is set out in your proposal.

Can I combine or customize packages?

Yes. Tell us what you need and we'll build a proposal around it, using these packages as a guide.

Do you guarantee results?

No one can honestly guarantee rankings, sales or ad returns. We guarantee to deliver the agreed work to a professional standard and to explain what we did and why.

How do we get started?

Book a consultation or message us on WhatsApp. We'll learn about your store, recommend a package and send a written proposal.
